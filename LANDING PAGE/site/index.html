How it works

From a pile of CVs to a shortlist

You already have the CVs. We don’t go looking for
people — we tell you which of yours are worth your time.

  1. Phase 01

    You set
    the benchmark

    • The CVs are already yours —From your job ad, careers page or inbox. We don’t search LinkedIn and we don’t go looking for people.
    • Show it who’s good —Upload two to five CVs of people already on your team who do this job well. You write nothing about them.

  2. Phase 02

    The engine
    reads them

    • It finds what they share —Not keywords. Things like “both grew into the role from support” — patterns you’d never put in a job ad.
    • You see what it found —Each trait weighted, with the exact line from the CV it came from. Nothing unexplained.

  3. Phase 03

    Every CV,
    ranked

    • Drop in the pile —Up to 200 CVs at once. PDF, Word or text. Minutes later they’re ranked, best first.
    • A reason for every one —“Matches 7 of 9 — ran a team of five, ten years in payments.” Citing their own CV, not a black-box score.

  4. Phase 04

    You decide,
    we remember

    • You read the top ten —Not all 200. The engine ranks; every hiring decision stays yours.
    • Record what happened —Mark who you interviewed and hired, and the engine calibrates on your real outcomes.
