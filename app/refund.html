The short version: 14-day money-back guarantee — request a refund within 14 days of any charge and we'll refund you in full, no questions asked. Cancel anytime. No conditions, no fine print.

Free trial

Every paid plan starts with a free trial. We ask for a payment method up front so your subscription can continue seamlessly if you decide to stay. You can use all features of your chosen plan during the trial, and we'll email you before it ends. Cancel any time before the trial ends and you are not charged anything. If you don't cancel, the trial automatically converts to a paid subscription and your card is charged for the first period — and you're still covered by our 14-day money-back guarantee below.

Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time — no notice period, no cancellation fee, no awkward phone call required. Just go to your billing settings and click cancel.

  • When you cancel, you keep access to your plan until the end of the billing period you've already paid for
  • After that, your account switches to a free read-only state — your data stays, nothing is deleted
  • You can reactivate at any time by adding a payment method

Refunds

We offer a straightforward 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not happy for any reason, email us within 14 days of a charge and we'll refund you in full — no questions asked. Duplicate charges are always refunded immediately.

Your data after cancellation

When you cancel, your account data is retained for 90 days in case you change your mind. After 90 days, your data is permanently deleted from our systems. If you want it deleted sooner, email us at hello@aitmp.io and we'll handle it within 30 days.
